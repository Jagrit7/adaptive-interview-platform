# Behavioural round - STAR method

Q: Tell me about a time you disagreed with a decision your team made.
A: Look for the specific position each side held and the evidence brought, not just "we talked it through". The strongest signal is disagreeing openly and then committing fully once the decision was made. Watch for candidates who only describe disagreements they won.

Q: Describe a project that failed. What was your part in it?
A: Wants a real failure with the candidate inside it, not a disguised success or a failure caused entirely by other people. Look for a specific decision they would make differently and evidence they changed their behaviour afterwards.

Q: Tell me about a time you had to give someone difficult feedback.
A: Should include what was actually said, roughly, and how the other person reacted. Look for feedback delivered close to the event and in private, and for the candidate checking their own read of the situation before delivering it.

Q: Give me an example of a time you had to learn something quickly to unblock your work.
A: Look for a concrete method: what they read, who they asked, what they built to test their understanding. The interesting part is how they knew when they knew enough to proceed safely.

Q: Describe a situation where you had to work with limited information.
A: Should show what assumptions they made explicit, which decisions they deferred until more was known, and what would have made them reverse course. Weak answers describe guessing confidently.

Q: Tell me about a time you improved a process rather than just doing the work.
A: Look for a problem they noticed themselves, evidence it was actually a problem and not just a preference, and whether the improvement survived after they moved on.
